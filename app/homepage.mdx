# Welcome to Blog App!

A cozy, minimalistic blog app built with Next.js, React, Drizzle ORM, and PostgreSQL.

## Features

- Browse blogs sorted by likes
- Search blogs by title
- Create an account and log in
- Publish new blog entries
- View user profiles and their submitted blogs
- Build a personal reading list
- Organize reading-list entries into unread and read sections

## Getting started

1. [Browse the blog collection](/blogs)
2. [Discover fellow blog enthusiasts](/users)
3. [Create and share new blogs](/blogs/new)
4. [View your personal profile](/me)

After registering and logging in, you can create blogs, show your appreciation by liking them, and maintain your own reading list.
